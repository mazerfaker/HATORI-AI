# Hatoriel visual identity
User preference: black as the primary color, a restrained burgundy secondary accent. Preserve readability; avoid teal as a default brand accent in the eventual redesign.
Proposed dark tokens (subject to visual review): background #09090B, surface #161417, elevated #201B1E, burgundy #68263A, accessible rose accent #CE8297, text #F4F0F2, secondary text #B5AAB0. Burgundy is for selected controls and accents, not large bright backgrounds.
0.4.1 introduces these tokens on Android. A comprehensive redesign of Windows, Android and future iOS, including avatar, logo, typography, animations and accessible contrast, remains a dedicated design task. No automatic asset replacement or rename of internal data paths.

## Approval gate (user requirement)
Before implementing the comprehensive redesign, present multiple visual concepts and interactive, clickable prototypes for Windows and Android for user review, including chat, memory, settings, navigation, avatar and responsive layout. Wait for explicit approval of the chosen concept before modifying the production UI, logo, avatar or global theme. Keep technical development separate. The current 0.4.1 palette is a provisional baseline, not an approved final design. Future iOS should follow the approved identity.
